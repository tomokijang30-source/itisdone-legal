# Privacy Policy

This policy explains how **It Is Done** (the "App"), operated by **Ring** (a sole proprietorship represented by Yuseung Jang, "we"), handles your information.

Effective date: **2026-11-25** (previous version: 2026-11-20)

## 1. Summary

- The App has **no sign-up or login.** We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos.
- Your affirmation, your name, your scene, the audio of your scene and your daily records are **stored on your iPhone.** We don't keep a copy on any server. Scene drafts are written on your device by Apple's on-device model (or by templates built into the App), so your words don't leave the device for that. (If iCloud Backup is on, this data is included in your Apple backup.)
- Data leaves your device in only two cases, and only when you choose it:
  1. **Your scene in a narrator's voice.** If you tap **Send and listen**, the text of your scene goes through our relay server to Typecast, a speech service run by Neosapience. Below, "scene text" means the scene and the affirmation you wrote. If you tap **Just show the text**, nothing is sent and your scene and affirmation appear as text on screen.
  2. **Backups you export.** In **Settings > Back Up & Restore**, exporting creates one file with your scene, records and audio, and the share sheet sends it wherever you pick (Files, AirDrop, Mail and so on). We never receive that file.
- We don't use advertising SDKs or App Tracking Transparency, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
- **Anonymous usage data and error reports** are sent **only if you tap Allow** in the App. If you decline, every feature and purchase works exactly the same.
- Anonymous usage data never includes what you type or the content of your onboarding answers. A few events carry only a yes/no value (for example, whether a grey example word ended up in your answer) or a length range (for example, how long a rewritten scene is).

## 2. What we process and why

| Category | Data | Purpose | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anonymous usage data | A random anonymous ID; a fixed set of events (such as onboarding progress and viewing the purchase screen) and their values (such as step number, yes/no values, length ranges, purchased product, price and currency); app version; device type and OS version; language; approximate country/city derived from IP address | Find where people get stuck and improve the App | **Only if you consented** |
| Error reports | Error name and message (only the name, if the message is long or could contain your input), code location, app and OS version | Find and fix crashes and bugs | **Only if you consented** |
| Purchase status | Anonymous purchaser ID; purchase and subscription history (product, period, trial, renewal/cancellation status); store country | Unlock what you bought and restore purchases | When you buy or restore (independent of the consent switch) |
| Support email | Your email address and message, plus the app version and build, iOS version, device model and language added at the bottom of the email | Answer your message | When you email us from Settings > Contact Us |
| Scene voice | The text of your scene (written by you), its language, a random device ID used for a daily limit, your IP address | Turn your scene into an audio file saved on your device | **Only if you allow it** in the App, when your scene is first created or changes |

- Payment details (such as card numbers) are handled by Apple. We never receive them.
- A backup file is created on your device and goes only where you send it, so we don't process it. It contains your own words and audio; keep it the way you would keep a private document.
- We do not try to identify you personally from this data.

- To keep this data from leaving your device, turn off the usage-data switch and the voice switch in Settings. Purchase status is required for paid features.

## 4. Retention and deletion

- Anonymous usage data and error reports: kept for **1 year**, then deleted automatically in PostHog.
- Purchase status: kept while your purchase or subscription is active and for any period required by law (e.g. 5 years for payment records under Korean consumer protection law).
- Support emails: kept for **1 year** after we reply, then deleted.
- Scene text: not kept by us or by the relay server. The daily counts (hashed values) are deleted automatically after **2 days**.
- Data on your device (affirmation, scene, records, audio files, consent choices) is deleted when you delete the App. A backup file you exported stays where you saved it until you delete it.
- Deletion method: electronic files are deleted so they cannot be recovered. We keep no paper records.

## 5. Withdrawing consent and your rights

- **Usage data:** In the App, go to **Settings > Send anonymous usage data** and turn it off. Sending stops immediately. Turn it back on to resume. Everything, including purchases, works the same either way.
- **Scene voice:** In the App, go to **Settings > Voice > Use a narrator's voice** and turn it off. From then on your scene text is not sent and your scene appears as text. Everything, including purchases, works the same.
- You may ask to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your data by emailing us (section 7). We will respond without delay (within 10 days). Because data is stored only under a random anonymous ID, we may be unable to find data that we cannot link to you. Deleting the App removes the anonymous ID from your device, so future data is not linked to past data.
- Subscriptions are cancelled and refunded through Apple (iPhone Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions, or Settings > Manage subscription in the App; refunds at <https://reportaproblem.apple.com>).

## 6. Cookies, children, security

- The App does not use cookies. Usage data is grouped by a random ID created on your device and is not sent if you decline.
- The App is not directed to children under 14 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.
- Security: encrypted transfer (HTTPS), least-privilege access with two-factor authentication on provider accounts, and collecting only what we need.
