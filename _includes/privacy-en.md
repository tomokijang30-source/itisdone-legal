# Privacy Policy

This policy explains how **It Is Done** (the "App"), operated by **Ring** (a sole proprietorship represented by Yuseung Jang, "we"), handles your information.

Effective date: **2026-11-20**

## 1. Summary

- The App has **no sign-up or login.** We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos.
- The affirmations, names and scenes you write, the recordings of your voice and your daily records are **stored only on your iPhone.** We never receive them. The writing helper runs on Apple's on-device model, so your words do not leave the device. (If iCloud Backup is on, they may be included in your Apple backup.)
- We do not use advertising SDKs or App Tracking Transparency, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
- **Anonymous usage data and error reports** are sent **only if you tap Allow** in the App. If you decline, every feature and purchase works exactly the same.
- A few of your onboarding choices (for example, the kind of goal you picked) may be included in anonymous usage data, and only if you allowed it. Nothing you type is included.

## 2. What we process and why

| Category | Data | Purpose | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anonymous usage data | A random anonymous ID; a fixed set of events (such as onboarding progress and viewing the purchase screen) and their values (such as step number, kind of goal chosen, purchased product, price and currency); app version; device type and OS version; language; approximate country/city derived from IP address | Find where people get stuck and improve the App | **Only if you consented** |
| Error reports | Error name and message (only the name, if the message is long or could contain your input), code location, app and OS version | Find and fix crashes and bugs | **Only if you consented** |
| Purchase status | Anonymous purchaser ID; purchase and subscription history (product, period, trial, renewal/cancellation status); store country | Unlock what you bought and restore purchases | When you buy or restore (independent of the consent switch) |
| Support email | Your email address and message (including a note you send from "It came true"), plus the app version and build, iOS version, device model and language added at the bottom of the email | Answer your message | When you email us |

- Payment details (such as card numbers) are handled by Apple. We never receive them.
- We do not try to identify you personally from this data.

## 3. Service providers and international transfer

We use the following service providers. Each protects your data under its own privacy policy and contractual terms (such as a data processing agreement).

| Recipient | Purpose | Data | Location · timing · method | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PostHog, Inc. | Store and analyze anonymous usage data and errors | Usage data and error reports (section 2) | United States · while you use the App · encrypted over the internet | Section 4 |

- Their policies: PostHog <https://posthog.com/privacy>, RevenueCat <https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy>, Apple <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>, Google <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- To stop usage data and error reports from leaving your device, turn off the switch in Settings. Purchase status is required for paid features.

## 4. Retention and deletion

- Anonymous usage data and error reports: kept for **1 year**, then deleted automatically in PostHog.
- Purchase status: kept while your purchase or subscription is active and for any period required by law (e.g. 5 years for payment records under Korean consumer protection law).
- Support emails: kept for **1 year** after we reply, then deleted.
- Data stored on your device (affirmations, records, recordings, consent choice) is deleted when you delete the App.
- Deletion method: electronic files are deleted so they cannot be recovered. We keep no paper records.

## 5. Withdrawing consent and your rights

- **Withdraw consent:** In the App, go to **Settings > Send anonymous usage data** and turn it off. Sending stops immediately. Turn it back on to resume. Everything, including purchases, works the same either way.
- You may ask to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your data by emailing us (section 7). We will respond without delay (within 10 days). Because data is stored only under a random anonymous ID, we may be unable to find data that we cannot link to you. Deleting the App removes the anonymous ID from your device, so future data is not linked to past data.
- Subscriptions are cancelled and refunded through Apple (iPhone Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions; refunds at <https://reportaproblem.apple.com>).

## 6. Cookies, children, security

- The App does not use cookies. Usage data is grouped by a random ID created on your device and is not sent if you decline.
- The App is not directed to children under 14 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.
- Security: encrypted transfer (HTTPS), least-privilege access with two-factor authentication on provider accounts, and collecting only what we need.
