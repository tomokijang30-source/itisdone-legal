# Support

**It Is Done** — questions, problems, or a note about something that came true:
email **<tomokijang30@gmail.com>**. You can write in any language.

- **Restore a purchase:** Settings › Restore Purchases, with the same Apple Account you bought with.
- **Cancel a subscription:** iPhone Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions, or Settings › Manage Subscription in the app.
- **Refunds:** Apple handles them at <https://reportaproblem.apple.com>.
- **Your data:** your scene and records are kept on your iPhone, and deleting the app deletes them. Only if you turn on the narrator's voice is your scene text sent to Typecast to make the audio (see the Privacy Policy).
- [Privacy Policy](../privacy/?lang=en)
